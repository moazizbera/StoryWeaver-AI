## 🚀 30-Second Quick Demo Script

### **Your narrative:**
> "StoryWeaver AI uses Google Gemini 2.5 Flash to generate complete cinematic storyboards. Let me show you a pre-generated story that demonstrates all capabilities."

### **Actions (follow exactly):**

1. **Open**: http://localhost:3001
2. **Wait**: 3 seconds for splash screen
3. **Scroll down** to cyan "Instant Demo" card
4. **Click**: "⚡ Load Instant Demo (0.5s) ⚡" button
5. **Show results** (auto-scrolls to top):

   **Overview Section:**
   - Title: "The Last Lighthouse"
   - Logline, genre, themes, runtime
   - Visual style specifications
   
   **Characters (scroll down):**
   - Marcus Chen (protagonist)
   - Lyra (deuteragonist) 
   - The Ocean's Voice (symbolic)
   - Show personality, arcs, visual descriptions
   
   **Cinematic Scenes (scroll down):**
   - Click Scene 1, 2, 3... arrows
   - **Show images** (lighthouse, ocean scenes)
   - Point out: dialogue, camera angles, lighting specs
   - **Copy illustration prompt** (hover and click button)
   
   **Storyboard (scroll down):**
   - Show shot types (WS, MS, CU)
   - Camera angles, movements
   - Composition notes
   
   **Export (scroll down):**
   - Click "📥 Export Story"
   - Show JSON, Markdown, Text options
   
   **Showcase Mode (click button):**
   - Enter presentation mode
   - Show full-screen view
   
   **History (top right):**
   - Click "📚 View History"
   - Show saved stories panel

6. **Close**: 
   > "That's StoryWeaver AI - production-ready cinematic narratives in seconds, powered by Google Gemini."
