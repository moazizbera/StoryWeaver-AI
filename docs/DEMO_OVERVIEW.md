# StoryWeaver AI - Demo Overview

## What is StoryWeaver AI?

StoryWeaver AI is an AI-powered cinematic storytelling platform that transforms a simple story concept into a complete, production-ready multimodal story package in approximately 30 seconds.

**Powered by:** Google Gemini 2.5 Flash  
**Built for:** Google Gemini Live Agent Challenge 2026

## The Problem

Traditional cinematic storytelling and pre-production is a time-intensive, multi-step process:

- **Writers** spend hours developing characters, plotting scenes, and writing dialogue
- **Directors** need detailed storyboards with camera angles, lighting notes, and shot specifications
- **Visual artists** require comprehensive scene descriptions for concept art and visualization
- **Producers** need structured content for planning, budgeting, and resource allocation

Current AI writing tools generate plain text narratives but lack:
- Structured production specifications
- Cinematic technical details (camera work, lighting, composition)
- Visual design guidelines
- Character depth with emotional arcs
- Shot-by-shot storyboard breakdowns

## The Solution

StoryWeaver AI generates a **complete production package** from a single prompt, including:

### 1. Story Foundation
- Professional title and logline
- Genre classification
- Thematic elements
- Estimated runtime
- Visual style guidelines with color grading and art direction

### 2. Character Development
- Fully-developed character profiles
- Personality traits and motivations
- Visual descriptions for concept artists
- Character arcs and emotional journeys
- Role definitions (protagonist, antagonist, supporting, mentor)

### 3. Cinematic Scenes
- Scene-by-scene narrative breakdown
- Dialogue scripted for each scene
- Mood and emotional tone specifications
- Camera angles and shot types
- Lighting design and color palette
- AI-ready illustration prompts for each scene

### 4. Production Storyboard
- Shot-by-shot technical specifications
- Duration estimates for each shot
- Visual notes for cinematography
- Audio design notes (sound effects, music cues)
- Camera movement descriptions

### 5. Narration Layer
- Voice-over scripts for each scene
- Emotional delivery guidance
- Pacing recommendations
